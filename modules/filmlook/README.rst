filmlook
========

Film look simulation for Canon EOS 5D Mark II: one full-screen panel under TRASH,
Lightroom / Camera Raw sidecars (.xmp) for every photo, optional finished JPG (FILMLOOK folder).
Every film keeps its own slider changes in ML/SETTINGS/FILMLOOK.TXT; the films themselves are never rewritten.
