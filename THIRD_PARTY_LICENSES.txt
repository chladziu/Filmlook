The JPG engine (modules/filmlook/engine/ and develop.h, lookbuild.h) is derived from "Soup Engine"
(https://github.com/jiijii0/soup-engine, author: luigi manga), licensed under the GNU General Public License,
version 2. What was taken: the baseline JPEG decoder with half-size decoding and streaming, the JPEG encoder,
the CR2 preview finder idea and the integer colour pipeline (curves, per-hue colour, split toning, grain,
halation, vignette). What was changed: floating point and 64-bit arithmetic were replaced by integer code,
the EXIF writer was made integer-only, halation / vignette / hue tables were adapted, and the film data
and the panel are new. The complete licence text is in modules/filmlook/engine/LICENSE.
The module as a whole is therefore distributed under the GPL (version 2), like Magic Lantern itself.
